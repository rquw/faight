How to play

Faight is an infinite, round based 2D brawler for people who want to play with their friends during school without any risk. Up to 20 players share a room. Your goal is simple: be the last one standing — punch the others to death or shoot them with the weapons that keep falling from the sky. Every round starts over on a new map, so you can tab out whenever a teacher walks past and nothing is lost.

Move with A and D, jump with Space (you get a second, weaker jump in mid air), crouch or drop faster with S, aim with the mouse, shoot by clicking and throw your weapon with Q. Press Enter to chat.

On a phone: the left thumb is a stick that appears wherever you put it — drag sideways to walk, down to crouch, flick up to jump. The right thumb aims: drag to line up the shot (you see exactly where it goes), let go to fire. Assault rifle and minigun keep firing while you hold the stick out, and a quick tap shoots at whoever is closest.

Impact and footstep sounds by Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0.

Heads up: the lag can get noticeable, especially with a lot of people online — this runs on a free server because nobody earns anything here. Donations welcome ;)
